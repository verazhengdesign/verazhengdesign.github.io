General

Hi! I’m Vera, a UX designer and researcher based in Toronto. I studied Information (User Experience Design) at the University of Toronto, and before that Finance at the University of Alberta, which is probably why I’m as comfortable in a spreadsheet as in a Figma file：）

I like the messy middle of design: the interviews, the card sorts, the patterns that only appear once you’ve talked to enough people. Give me a hard question and a blank canvas and I’m genuinely happy.

I’ve worked as a design researcher at RBC on their online banking platform, and I care most about the moments where research actually changes what gets built.
