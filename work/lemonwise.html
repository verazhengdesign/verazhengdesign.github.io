Overview

What This Project Is

LemonWise is a mobile app that helps young adults — especially university students — improve their financial literacy and build better money-management habits.

Designed over a semester with a team of four, from interviews through to a tested, high-fidelity prototype.

Context

The Problem

Many students struggle with budgeting, saving, and understanding basic financial concepts, which can lead to poor financial decisions and long-term consequences.

“I just graduated and I’m making my own money for the first time. What do I do now?!”
Problem statementHelping new grads find non-intimidating ways to invest early on, so they can build financial literacy and nurture healthy money habits for long-term success.
Solution statementEmpower new grads with tools to set clear financial goals, visualize spending habits, and access actionable advice, fostering financial independence through guided investments and rewarding learning incentives.
Research

Research Methodology

My team and I conducted semi-structured interviews with 8 participants aged 21–25, all financially independent students, to understand the behaviours and struggles behind student money management.

8Interviewees
21–25Years old
15–20Minutes

The user research plan employed interviews to thoroughly understand participants’ financial experiences and their impact on money management.

Key areas of focus:

Understanding how students, particularly those who are financially independent, manage their finances, and what resources or tools they currently use.

Interview Questions

Top 3 research questions:

  1. Do you have any experience using online financial management apps? If so, how was your experience?
  2. What would you do if you realized you were spending more than you had budgeted?
  3. Do you have any specific financial goals to achieve in the future?

Design

Design Approach

I turned the research into four high-level pillars — the goals every design decision had to serve — then moved from wireframes to a second usability test, iteration, and final mock-ups.

Spending Analytics

Deliver real-time spending analytics to help users gain more financial awareness, make informed decision.

Investment Integration

Create a seamless and intuitive experience that allows users to invest their money effectively while simultaneously working toward their financial goal.

Goal-Setting

Allows users to set financial goals in clear categories, factoring in timelines and amounts that align with their lifestyle.

Learning Hub

Give users opportunity to learn including videos, articles, and interactive quizzes.
