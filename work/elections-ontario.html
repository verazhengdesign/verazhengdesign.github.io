Overview

Project Overview

This project focused on improving Elections Ontario’s internship program by designing a structured onboarding and feedback experience through service blueprinting, frontstage and backstage process mapping, and physical prototyping to clarify intern responsibilities and strengthen ongoing support.

Problem

The Problem

At the request of our client, Elections Ontario, we identified a lack of a structured system to support intern onboarding, task clarity, and ongoing feedback. This resulted in limited guidance for interns and prevented Elections Ontario from fully leveraging interns’ contributions and long-term talent development potential.
